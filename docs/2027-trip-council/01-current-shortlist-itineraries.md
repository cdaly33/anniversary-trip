## 1. Northern Italy + Croatia

**Chosen timing:** September 9–20, 2027 (provisional)  
**Duration:** 12 days / 11 nights  
**Bases:** Lake Como — 4 nights; Venice — 3 nights; Rovinj — 4 nights

- **Day 1 — Arrive Lake Como:** Settle in, lakefront walk, early dinner.
- **Day 2 — Lake orientation:** Ferry-linked village exploration and a long, unhurried lunch.
- **Day 3 — Anniversary anchor:** Scenic lake day with a villa or garden visit, followed by a celebratory dinner.
- **Day 4 — Slow lake day:** Choose one additional town or a gentle scenic outing; preserve the afternoon for rest.
- **Day 5 — Transfer to Venice:** Travel to Venice, check in, and take an evening neighborhood walk.
- **Day 6 — Historic Venice:** Major landmark area in the morning; quiet lanes and canals after lunch.
- **Day 7 — Lagoon character:** Choose one lagoon-island excursion or a food-focused Venice day, not both.
- **Day 8 — Transfer to Rovinj:** Cross-border surface transfer and relaxed harbor evening (routing provisional).
- **Day 9 — Rovinj:** Old town, waterfront, and open time for swimming or café time.
- **Day 10 — Istrian interior:** Hill town and regional food or wine experience (operation provisional).
- **Day 11 — Adriatic pause:** Light coastal outing, final celebratory meal, and no major sightseeing agenda.
- **Day 12 — Depart:** Use the most practical regional gateway once 2027 schedules are known.

**Why this shape works:** Three substantial stays create a clear lake–historic city–Adriatic progression without one-night stops or an intra-trip flight. The anniversary moment lands early, while two intentionally lighter days keep the cross-border journey from turning the trip into a checklist.

## 3. Portugal

**Decision:** Choose **Portugal alone**, rather than Paris + Portugal. Paris would add a flight and hotel change while weakening the depth and downtime that make this the shortlist’s strongest value-and-comfort option.

**Chosen timing:** September 23–October 3, 2027 (provisional)  
**Duration:** 11 days / 10 nights  
**Bases:** Lisbon — 5 nights; Porto — 5 nights

- **Day 1 — Arrive Lisbon:** Settle in and take a gentle neighborhood walk.
- **Day 2 — Historic Lisbon:** One major historic district, viewpoints, and an unhurried food evening.
- **Day 3 — Sintra:** Full-day palaces, gardens, and town time; exact sites and operations provisional.
- **Day 4 — Lisbon at an easier pace:** Market or museum, followed by a free afternoon.
- **Day 5 — Coast or countryside:** Choose one scenic day trip; return for a relaxed final Lisbon dinner.
- **Day 6 — Transfer to Porto:** Rail north (2027 schedule provisional), check in, riverside orientation.
- **Day 7 — Historic Porto:** Old city and riverfront, with time for cafés and wandering.
- **Day 8 — Anniversary anchor:** Douro scenery and regional food or wine experience (operation provisional), followed by a celebratory dinner.
- **Day 9 — Northern history:** Guimarães or Braga day trip, chosen rather than combined.
- **Day 10 — Porto flex day:** Gardens, river outing, food experience, or restorative downtime.
- **Day 11 — Depart:** Travel home from the most practical confirmed gateway.

**Why this shape works:** Two five-night bases deliver castles and palaces, historic cities, coast or countryside, river scenery, and strong food without unnecessary flights. The unhurried Lisbon-to-Porto contrast gives Portugal enough scale and occasion to feel anniversary-worthy while preserving the best comfort and value profile of the three concepts.
