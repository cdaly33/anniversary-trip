## Portugal

**Status copy:** **Provisional leading fit.** Best current blend of comfort, distinctiveness, pace, and value, but still dependent on a satisfying Douro anniversary day and a practical Porto departure.

**Working season and shape:** Late September–early October 2027; Lisbon 5 nights + Porto 5 nights.

### Expected flight pattern from STL

Test an open-jaw itinerary, **STL–Lisbon (LIS) / Porto (OPO)–STL**. A plausible itinerary usually has one connection each way through a U.S. or European gateway; Porto may require two connections on weaker dates. If a relevant STL transatlantic service operates, London or Frankfurt could be examples; Newark, Washington Dulles, Philadelphia, Boston, New York JFK, or Miami are other examples to test, not promises. Allow roughly **11–17 hours one way**, ordinary connection time included. The outbound is overnight and arrives the next calendar day.

Prefer one protected ticket per direction and reject an airport change unless deliberately buffered. If OPO is poor, use the fallback: rail Porto–Lisbon on the penultimate day, an airport-area Lisbon night, then LIS–STL. Do not rely on a same-day Porto train feeding a long-haul departure. Sources: [STL air-service overview](https://www.flystl.com/business/commercial-development/passenger-air-service-development/), [Lisbon Metro](https://www.metrolisboa.pt/en/), [Metro do Porto maps and timetables](https://en.metrodoporto.pt/pages/396).

### Final itinerary

#### Travel Day 0 — Depart STL

- **Main idea:** Begin the overnight international itinerary to Lisbon.
- **Companion idea:** None; meals, hydration, movement, and sleep are the plan.
- **Why it fits:** Makes the true calendar burden visible and protects the arrival day.
- **Pace / terrain:** Airport walking, a connection, and prolonged sitting.
- **Fallback:** If disrupted, discard Day 1 orientation rather than borrow from sleep.
- **Links:** [STL air-service overview](https://www.flystl.com/business/commercial-development/passenger-air-service-development/).

#### Day 1 — Lisbon arrival and recovery · Lisbon night 1

- **Main idea:** Airport transfer, check-in, and a short Baixa/Praça do Comércio orientation only if energy permits.
- **Companion idea:** Early Portuguese meal near the hotel or a seated Tagus-side pause.
- **Why it fits:** Immediate regional atmosphere without turning an overnight-flight day into a checklist.
- **Pace / terrain:** Very light; choose the level riverfront and avoid optional hills.
- **Fallback:** Hotel meal and sleep.
- **Links:** [Visit Lisboa](https://www.visitlisboa.com/en), [Lisbon Metro](https://www.metrolisboa.pt/en/).

#### Day 2 — Historic Lisbon and local food · Lisbon night 2

- **Main idea:** Alfama and the castle district, starting uphill by transit and descending selectively.
- **Companion idea:** Fado Museum or Lisboa Story Centre, followed by a focused food evening.
- **Why it fits:** Layered history, neighborhood character, and food echo favorite qualities of Rome and Edinburgh.
- **Pace / terrain:** Moderate; steep grades, cobbles, and steps require rides and a seated break.
- **Fallback:** One historic interior and nearby dinner.
- **Links:** [São Jorge Castle](https://castelodesaojorge.pt/en/), [Museu do Fado](https://www.museudofado.pt/en), [Lisboa Story Centre](https://www.lisboastorycentre.pt/en).

#### Day 3 — Sintra palace day · Lisbon night 3

- **Main idea:** Pena Palace and park as the single timed anchor.
- **Companion idea:** Monserrate Palace and gardens only as the lighter second stop; do not stack another marquee palace.
- **Why it fits:** Portugal’s clearest palace-and-castle day and a visually memorable anniversary-trip experience.
- **Pace / terrain:** High-moderate; slopes, stairs, queues, shuttles, and uneven paths.
- **Fallback:** Favor the most weather-resilient interior and shorten the gardens; severe weather becomes a Lisbon museum day.
- **Links:** [Pena Palace](https://www.parquesdesintra.pt/en/parks-monuments/park-and-national-palace-of-pena/), [official Pena ticket guidance](https://www.parquesdesintra.pt/en/plan-your-visit/tickets-palace-of-pena/), [Monserrate](https://www.parquesdesintra.pt/en/parks-monuments/park-and-palace-of-monserrate/).

#### Day 4 — Easier Lisbon and free afternoon · Lisbon night 4

- **Main idea:** National Tile Museum for a compact, indoor cultural anchor.
- **Companion idea:** A genuinely free afternoon; Belém is optional only if energy is good.
- **Why it fits:** Adds Portuguese visual history while protecting the preferred breathing-room rhythm.
- **Pace / terrain:** Light and adjustable; Belém is flatter but involves longer distances.
- **Fallback:** Drop the formal visit and keep cafés, laundry, a nap, or independent wandering.
- **Links:** [National Tile Museum](https://www.museusemonumentos.pt/en/museus-e-monumentos/national-tile-museum), [UNESCO — Belém monuments](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/263/).

#### Day 5 — One Lisbon-region contrast · Lisbon night 5

- **Main idea:** Choose Évora for concentrated history **or** Cascais for coast and recovery.
- **Companion idea:** Final Lisbon dinner in a new neighborhood.
- **Why it fits:** Adds either Roman/walled-city depth or scenic contrast without a third base.
- **Pace / terrain:** Moderate; Évora has uneven paving, while the coast is exposed to wind and sun.
- **Fallback:** Calouste Gulbenkian Museum and a long Lisbon lunch.
- **Links:** [UNESCO — Évora](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/361/), [Visit Cascais](https://www.visitcascais.com/en), [Gulbenkian Museum](https://gulbenkian.pt/museu/en/).

#### Day 6 — Rail to Porto · Porto night 1

- **Main idea:** Intercity rail, hotel check-in, and luggage reset.
- **Companion idea:** Brief Ribeira orientation or São Bento’s tiled hall if convenient.
- **Why it fits:** One straightforward move earns a meaningfully different northern base.
- **Pace / terrain:** Transfer day; Porto’s hills, stairs, and slick paving increase luggage effort.
- **Fallback:** Direct hotel arrival and nearby dinner.
- **Links:** [Visit Porto](https://visitporto.travel/en-GB), [UNESCO — Historic Centre of Oporto](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/755/).

#### Day 7 — Historic Porto · Porto night 2

- **Main idea:** Palácio da Bolsa guided interior.
- **Companion idea:** Cathedral-to-river sequence or a lower-walking riverfront version with a café stop.
- **Why it fits:** Ceremonial architecture, lived-in history, food, and independent exploration.
- **Pace / terrain:** Moderate; start high and work downhill, then use transport back.
- **Fallback:** One interior and a covered food experience.
- **Links:** [Palácio da Bolsa](https://palaciodabolsa.com/en/), [Visit Porto](https://visitporto.travel/en-GB).

#### Day 8 — Anniversary Douro day · Porto night 3

- **Main idea:** A defined small-group or private Douro landscape day centered on scenery, river, and regional food.
- **Companion idea:** Special dinner, kept simple enough that the day is not an endurance test.
- **Why it fits:** Supplies Portugal’s singular landscape-and-food occasion; wine is optional, not the purpose.
- **Pace / terrain:** Mostly seated transport with selected viewpoints; avoid stacking long road time, multiple tastings, and a long cruise.
- **Fallback:** Serralves Museum, sheltered river views, and the anniversary dinner in Porto.
- **Links:** [Visit Portugal — Douro](https://www.visitportugal.com/en/content/douro-valley), [UNESCO — Alto Douro](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/1046/), [Serralves](https://www.serralves.pt/en/).

#### Day 9 — Northern Portugal history · Porto night 4

- **Main idea:** Guimarães historic center and castle zone.
- **Companion idea:** One regional lunch; Braga/Bom Jesus is an alternative, never an additional stop.
- **Why it fits:** Adds concentrated medieval and early-national history with Rachel-specific castle content.
- **Pace / terrain:** Moderate; old paving and grades require a shortened core route.
- **Fallback:** Porto museum, historic interior, or food experience.
- **Links:** [Visit Guimarães](https://www.visitguimaraes.travel/), [UNESCO — Guimarães](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/1031/), [UNESCO — Bom Jesus](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/1590/).

#### Day 10 — Porto flex and recovery · Porto night 5

- **Main idea:** No required booking; move a weather-affected anchor, revisit a favorite area, or rest.
- **Companion idea:** Slow Serralves visit or short river/promenade experience if energy is good.
- **Why it fits:** Protects the trip from rain and excursion fatigue.
- **Pace / terrain:** Fully adjustable, including zero formal activity.
- **Fallback:** Rest, pack, and eat nearby.
- **Links:** [Serralves](https://www.serralves.pt/en/), [Visit Porto](https://visitporto.travel/en-GB).

#### Day 11 — Porto departure and return to STL

- **Main idea:** Airport transfer and international return, normally one connection but possibly two.
- **Companion idea:** None.
- **Why it fits:** A travel-only final day prevents a missed connection or stressful nominal sight.
- **Pace / terrain:** Luggage, airport walking, and 11–17 hours of expected travel burden.
- **Fallback:** If OPO routing fails the booking gate, revise to a Lisbon positioning night and disclose the added night/count.
- **Links:** [Metro do Porto maps and timetables](https://en.metrodoporto.pt/pages/396), [STL air-service overview](https://www.flystl.com/business/commercial-development/passenger-air-service-development/).

## Northern Italy + Croatia

**Status copy:** **Conditional on routing.** A romantic, high-wow concept that advances only if Venice–Rovinj and the final regional gateway are verified as humane, resilient, and compatible with the stated nights.

**Working season and shape:** September 2027; Lake Como 4 nights + Venice 3 nights + Rovinj 4 nights.

### Expected flight pattern from STL

Use **Milan Malpensa (MXP)** inbound, normally with one connection through London, Frankfurt, or a U.S. gateway. Allow roughly **12–20 hours** to MXP plus the airport-to-lake transfer. The Croatia-side departure remains unresolved: test **Pula (PUY), Trieste (TRS), Venice (VCE), and Zagreb (ZAG)**. ## Verify before booking

1. Recheck exact 2027 flight operating dates with the airport and airline; treat current route pages only as evidence of plausible patterns.
2. Prefer one protected ticket per direction; identify every self-transfer, airport change, terminal change, baggage reclaim, and passport-control point.
3. Compare hotel-to-hotel elapsed time and recovery cost, not only fare or airborne time.
4. Recheck rail, ferry, road-transfer, attraction, excursion, and timed-entry operating dates for the exact weekday.
5. Confirm lodging night totals after any airport positioning night or gateway change, then refresh the whole-trip SWAG.
6. Check mobility realities: hills, cobbles, bridges, stairs, boarding gaps, exposed walks, and luggage handling.
7. Confirm weather and cancellation fallbacks without sacrificing the protected recovery day.
8. Recheck government entry/transit requirements, passport validity, and official New Zealand/Australia electronic travel authorization guidance where applicable: [NZeTA](https://www.immigration.govt.nz/new-zealand-visas/visas/visa/nzeta), [Australia ETA](https://immi.homeaffairs.gov.au/visas/getting-a-visa/visa-listing/electronic-travel-authority-601).
9. Reopen every Wikimedia file page, reconfirm creator/license/attribution, download the original locally, and record modifications. Never copy from tourism, museum, social, map, or article pages without explicit compatible reuse rights.
10. Preserve the status language: inclusion is not selection, a provisional route is not a promised flight, and New Zealand + Australia is not a recommended shape.
