## Shared assumptions

All downstream work must use the following assumptions consistently:

- **Travelers:** two adults.
- **Origin:** STL.
- **Airfare:** economy unless a different cabin is clearly labeled as an optional comparison.
- **Lodging:** safe, comfortable, well-located midrange lodging; no luxury default.
- **Currency/year:** estimated **2027 USD**.
- **Trip length and pace:** generally 10–12 days, up to 14 only when justified; two bases preferred and three maximum; usually 1–2 meaningful experiences per day with breathing room.
- **Season:** any suitable date after March 2027 through December 31, 2027; use the strongest defensible window for each concept.
- **Provisional facts:** exact schedules, fares, room rates, taxes, operating dates, availability, entry rules, and prices are provisional.
- **Flights:** never describe a route as nonstop unless it is verified for the relevant airport pair and schedule period. Distinguish a through itinerary from a nonstop flight.
- **Links:** do not invent, infer, or publish untested URLs.
- **Images:** use only openly licensed or public-domain images with source, creator, and license attribution. A linked official tourism page may illustrate an idea, but its image must not be copied unless its license expressly permits reuse.
- **Recommendation:** no artifact may imply that a final trip has been chosen or booked.

## Standard for each retained concept

### 1. Flight pattern from STL

Provide a concise, evidence-aware pattern rather than a promised itinerary:

- likely arrival and departure airport(s);
- whether an open-jaw structure is being tested;
- plausible connection gateway(s) and the expected number of stops;
- outbound/return shape, including overnight travel and International Date Line effects where relevant;
- how the flight pattern connects to the first and last bases;
- material baggage, self-transfer, airport-change, or long-ground-transfer risks;
- a primary pattern and, where useful, one fallback pattern; and
- source links and the date checked.

Future schedules must be labeled provisional. Do not convert current seasonal service into a 2027 promise.

### 2. Day-level depth

Each numbered day should include:

- base and overnight location;
- one primary meaningful experience, or two compatible experiences at most;
- why the day matters to this couple or to the anniversary arc;
- a lighter, seated, lower-walking, weather, or recovery alternative when useful;
- meaningful transfer or reservation constraints;
- at least one working, trustworthy reference link where the day contains a factual attraction or route claim.

Prefer official attraction, government, UNESCO, national/regional tourism, transport-operator, museum, or other primary sources. Wikipedia may provide durable background, but it must not be the sole support for schedule, price, safety, transport, or entry claims.

Links should help a reader understand or verify the proposed experience, not turn each day into an exhaustive directory.

### 3. Pictures and attribution

Use a small, intentional visual set that helps distinguish the concepts and their anchor experiences.

For every locally displayed image, record:

- image title or description;
- creator, when supplied;
- source page URL;
- license name and license URL;
- any required attribution wording or modification notice.

Preferred sources include Wikimedia Commons file pages and government collections that explicitly identify public-domain or open-license status. A Wikipedia article thumbnail is not sufficient licensing evidence; verify the underlying file page. If reuse rights are unclear, link the official page without copying its image.

### 4. Whole-trip cost overview

Show one broad range for the entire concept and a short hotel-context note. Do not show day-by-day, hotel-by-hotel, or flight-by-flight prices.

The **whole-trip SWAG for two travelers** should include:

- economy airfare from and back to STL;
- the itinerary's stated hotel nights in safe, comfortable, well-located midrange lodging, including expected lodging taxes/mandatory fees where estimable;
- required rail, ferry, rental car, domestic/trans-Tasman flight, private transfer, or other transport needed to execute the displayed route;
- normal airport transfers and local transportation;
- representative admission fees and major displayed tours or excursions;
- ordinary meals, including one celebratory meal;
- a reasonable allowance for travel insurance and uncertainty/contingency.

It should exclude:

- passports or renewals;
- points/miles valuations or credit-card benefits;
- premium-economy, business-class, or luxury-hotel upgrades unless separately labeled;
- shopping, souvenirs, pet care, house care, medical expenses, and discretionary nightlife;
- optional splurges not necessary to execute the displayed itinerary.

State the estimate date, assumed trip length, broad lodging comfort level, important exchange-rate assumption, principal cost drivers, and confidence level. Use rounded ranges suitable for comparison, not false precision.

### Value

- Add concise lodging context for each base: appropriate midrange comfort, useful neighborhoods/areas, likely relative cost pressure, and what location tradeoff matters.

### Itinerary

### Visual Designer

### Rai

- Review the refreshed content for accessibility assumptions, cultural respect, sustainability, privacy, and overconfident safety/health/entry claims.
- Confirm alternatives do not assume swimming, alcohol, strenuous walking, or premium spending.
- Check that image reuse and attribution are responsible and that official-tourism links are not treated as permission to copy images.
- Issue a blocking finding for deceptive citations or known unsafe advice; otherwise attach practical advisories.

## Handoff order and gates

1. **Logistics, Destination Guide, and Value** prepare evidence-backed inputs.
2. **Itinerary** integrates them into four canonical daily plans.
3. **Fact Checker** verifies claims, links, estimates, and image licenses; **Rai** performs responsible-travel review.
4. **Visual Designer** finalizes the richer comparison hierarchy using verified content.
5. **Web Developer** implements only the approved four-concept package.
6. Run the existing **Itinerary Balance Review** before treating the daily plans as complete and **Pre-Share Verification** before publication.

Publication is blocked by broken or invented links, unverified image rights, unsupported nonstop claims, inconsistent cost assumptions, or any presentation that silently upgrades New Zealand + Australia from its existing structural-risk status.
