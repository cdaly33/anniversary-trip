# 2027 High-Level Cost Overview

_Planning estimate prepared August 23, 2026. These are broad comparison ranges, not quotes or booking targets. All 2027 fares, room rates, taxes, exchange rates, schedules, and availability remain provisional._

## Common basis and method

Each range covers **two adults traveling from and back to STL in economy**, using the council's established itinerary length: Portugal and Spain at **11 days / 10 hotel nights**, Northern Italy + Croatia at **12 days / 11 hotel nights**, and New Zealand + Australia at **14 calendar days / 11 hotel nights**. Lodging assumes a safe, comfortable, well-located independent or chain hotel at roughly solid 3-star to modest 4-star level, not luxury.

The whole-trip SWAG includes economy airfare, hotels and expected mandatory lodging charges, the major itinerary transfers, ordinary local transportation, representative paid anchors, normal meals plus one celebratory dinner, **international travel insurance**, and a prudent uncertainty allowance. It excludes passports, points or card-benefit valuations, premium-cabin and luxury upgrades, **shopping and souvenirs**, pet/house care, medical costs, discretionary nightlife, and optional splurges not needed to execute the itinerary.

Methodologically, current destination-spend and hotel averages were used as a floor and reasonableness check, then adjusted upward for the requested lodging standard and location, the displayed route's required transfers and anchors, 2027 price/currency uncertainty, and STL's connecting-flight reality. Budget Your Trip says its destination averages combine anonymized traveler submissions with travel-industry pricing data; they are useful benchmarks, not live quotes ([methodology](https://www.budgetyourtrip.com/about)). Current airfare metasearch was used only for broad market calibration ([Google Flights](https://www.google.com/travel/flights)); no individual fare is published here. Currency exposure was checked against Federal Reserve/FRED daily reference series for the [euro](https://fred.stlouisfed.org/series/DEXUSEU), [New Zealand dollar](https://fred.stlouisfed.org/series/DEXUSNZ), and [Australian dollar](https://fred.stlouisfed.org/series/DEXUSAL). The ranges intentionally absorb plausible movement rather than assume today's exchange rate will hold.

## Portugal

**Whole-trip SWAG:** **$7,500–$10,500 for two** in 2027 USD.

**Expected hotel band:** approximately **$180–$300 per night in Lisbon** and **$160–$270 in Porto**, including room-tax/fee breathing room. In Lisbon, this should usually buy a comfortable, well-reviewed room in or near Baixa, Chiado, Avenida, or a transit-convenient adjoining neighborhood; the tradeoff at the lower end is typically room size, hill exposure, or a short ride to the historic core. In Porto, it should buy a characterful midrange stay in Baixa, Cedofeita, or near—but not necessarily directly on—the Ribeira waterfront; river views and easy, level access command premiums.

**Cost pressure: Lower.** The total is most likely to move because of:

- STL–Portugal airfare and whether an open-jaw return from Porto prices reasonably;
- Lisbon's increasingly strong lodging demand and exact neighborhood;
- the scale and format of the Douro anniversary day;
- exchange-rate movement and September/October event compression.

**Confidence: Medium-high.** The two-base, one-rail-move shape is comparatively simple, and current traveler-spend benchmarks for [Lisbon](https://www.budgetyourtrip.com/portugal/lisbon) and [Porto](https://www.budgetyourtrip.com/portugal/porto) provide consistent calibration. Confidence stops short of high because 2027 air schedules and open-jaw pricing are not yet firm.

**Value verdict:** Portugal offers the strongest probability of a comfortable, distinctive anniversary trip without paying heavily for logistical complexity.

## Spain

**Whole-trip SWAG:** **$8,000–$11,000 for two** in 2027 USD.

**Expected hotel band:** approximately **$190–$310 per night in Madrid** and **$170–$280 in Seville**. In Madrid, this should generally secure a polished midrange room around Centro, Barrio de las Letras, or the Retiro/Salamanca edge, with the lowest prices more likely to mean a smaller room or a few additional metro stops. In Seville, it should buy an atmospheric, comfortable stay around Santa Cruz, El Arenal, central Casco Antiguo, or a quieter adjacent district; cathedral views, palace-style properties, and major-event dates can move well above the band.

**Cost pressure: Moderate.** The total is most likely to move because of:

- transatlantic airfare from STL and the final arrival/departure airport strategy;
- Madrid and Seville hotel demand around festivals, conferences, or major events;
- how many Toledo, Segovia, Córdoba, or countryside days become guided excursions;
- rail timing and the desired degree of centrality versus room size.

**Confidence: Medium-high.** The route needs only one straightforward intercity move, both cities have deep hotel supply, and [Madrid](https://www.budgetyourtrip.com/spain/madrid) and [Seville](https://www.budgetyourtrip.com/spain/seville) benchmarks are directionally coherent. Event-driven rate spikes remain the principal unknown.

**Value verdict:** Spain costs modestly more than Portugal but converts that premium into an especially strong history, royal-site, food, and independent-exploration fit.

## Northern Italy + Croatia

**Whole-trip SWAG:** **$10,500–$15,000 for two** in 2027 USD.

**Expected hotel band:** approximately **$250–$420 per night at Lake Como**, **$240–$400 in Venice**, and **$180–$310 in Rovinj**. At Como, the range favors Como town, Menaggio, or another ferry-useful base over trophy-view luxury; lakefront position and Bellagio-style scarcity cost more. In Venice, it should buy a comfortable room in Cannaregio, Dorsoduro, Santa Croce, or a quieter central pocket, but often with compact dimensions, stairs, or a walk from the vaporetto. In Rovinj, it should buy a strong midrange room near the old-town edge or harbor; direct sea views, parking, and resort amenities raise the rate.

**Cost pressure: Higher.** The total is most likely to move because of:

- premium September lodging at Lake Como and Venice;
- the still-unresolved Venice–Rovinj and Rovinj-to-departure-gateway solution;
- private transfers or a fallback routing if seasonal Adriatic service does not work;
- lake/lagoon transport, luggage handling, and exchange-rate movement.

**Confidence: Medium.** Current benchmarks confirm that both [Como](https://www.budgetyourtrip.com/italy/como) and [Venice](https://www.budgetyourtrip.com/italy/venice) carry substantial destination and lodging premiums, while the [Croatia-wide benchmark](https://www.budgetyourtrip.com/croatia) supports lower—but still seasonal—cost pressure in the final base. The unresolved cross-border transfer and departure gateway materially widen the estimate.

**Value verdict:** This can justify its premium through romance and scenic variety, but only if the Croatia transfers are solved without expensive private-transport improvisation.

## New Zealand + Australia

**Whole-trip SWAG:** **$14,000–$20,000 for two** in 2027 USD.

**Expected hotel band:** approximately **$230–$380 per night in Queenstown**, **$170–$290 in Te Anau**, and **$230–$390 in Sydney**. In Queenstown, this generally buys a comfortable central or near-central room, though lake views, peak dates, and easy walking access are expensive; Frankton can improve space/value at the cost of atmosphere and transit time. Te Anau should offer a clean motel, lodge, or modest hotel near town or the lake, with inventory scarcity more important than star labels. In Sydney, the band should buy a reliable room in the CBD, near the southern Rocks, or in a well-connected inner district; direct harbor views and landmark frontage are normally outside the value brief.

**Cost pressure: Highest.** The total is most likely to move because of:

- long-haul, multi-city airfare from STL and limited date flexibility;
- the Queenstown–Te Anau road transfer plus trans-Tasman flight;
- weather-sensitive Fiordland excursions and any replacement/rebooking cost;
- high-demand Queenstown and Sydney lodging, currency movement, and 14-day insurance exposure.

**Confidence: Medium-low.** [Queenstown](https://www.budgetyourtrip.com/new-zealand/queenstown), [Te Anau](https://www.budgetyourtrip.com/new-zealand/te-anau), and [Sydney](https://www.budgetyourtrip.com/australia/sydney) benchmarks establish the broad on-the-ground order of magnitude, but airfare, separate currencies, weather-dependent anchors, and the complicated road-air transfer create much more variance than the European concepts.

**Value verdict:** It delivers the largest statement-trip potential, but it is clearly the weakest usable-time-per-dollar proposition and remains structurally high-risk under the 14-day ceiling.

## Reading the ranges

Portugal and Spain have the most stable cost architecture: abundant city lodging and a single major intercity move. Northern Italy + Croatia has a visible premium plus unresolved transfer risk. New Zealand + Australia combines the highest airfare exposure, three bases, a road transfer, a trans-Tasman flight, and expensive scenic-city lodging. For all four, the financially meaningful next checkpoint is a like-for-like 2027 fare-and-hotel snapshot after schedules and bookable inventory open—not a tighter estimate today.
