## How to read this draft

- **Travel days from STL and post-trip recovery are protected**, not disguised as sightseeing days.
- European concepts retain their established destination shapes; the overnight outbound flight begins before Day 1.
- **`Flight pattern pending Logistics verification`** means no routing, stop count, gateway, or nonstop service is being promised.
- **`Logistics verification required`** marks a day whose displayed shape depends on transport timing, baggage handling, transfer length, or a workable fallback.
- Each destination day contains one main experience and, at most, one complementary idea. Flex time is intentionally part of the plan.
- Named places are itinerary candidates, not assertions about 2027 opening days or ticket availability.

**Status:** leading fit, but still conditional on a distinctive Douro celebration and practical Porto departure  
**STL air shape:** `flight pattern pending Logistics verification`; test STL–Lisbon inbound and Porto–STL outbound against a Lisbon round trip with a final rail return.

### Reserved travel and recovery

- **Outbound travel before Day 1:** Reserve the prior afternoon/evening and overnight for STL departure and international transit. No arrival-day booking should depend on an on-time flight.
- **Return and recovery:** Day 11 is reserved for the trip home. Protect the following calendar day at home for sleep, laundry, and schedule recovery.

### Day 1 — Arrive and settle into Lisbon

- **Base / overnight:** Lisbon, night 1 of 5.
- **Main experience:** Arrival, hotel check-in, and a gentle orientation in the immediate neighborhood.
- **Complementary idea:** Early Portuguese meal close to the hotel rather than crossing the city.
- **Why it fits Rachel and Chris:** It begins with regional character while protecting energy after overnight travel.
- **Pace / terrain:** Very light; keep hills and cobbles optional and use a ride if the hotel approach is steep.
- **Weather / energy fallback:** Check in, eat indoors, and sleep; the orientation walk is fully disposable.
- **Logistics verification required:** Confirm the inbound airport, connection pattern, airport-to-hotel plan, and realistic arrival window.

### Day 2 — Historic Lisbon and local food

- **Base / overnight:** Lisbon, night 2 of 5.
- **Main experience:** Explore one historic district and its principal church, civic, or viewpoint sequence.

- **Pace / terrain:** Moderate and hilly; use trams, metro, funiculars, or rides strategically and schedule a seated break.

### Day 3 — Sintra palace and garden day

- **Base / overnight:** Lisbon, night 3 of 5.

- **Weather / energy fallback:** Substitute a Porto museum, historic interior, or food experience and reclaim the afternoon.
- **Logistics verification required:** Confirm realistic station/transfer time and whether the selected city works comfortably as a single-focus day trip.

### Day 10 — Genuine Porto flex and recovery

- **Base / overnight:** Porto, night 5 of 5.

- **Complementary idea:** Optional garden, short river experience, or focused food activity only if energy is good.

### Day 11 — Porto departure and return to STL

- **Base / overnight:** In transit / home; no hotel night planned.
- **Main experience:** Airport transfer and international return travel.
- **Complementary idea:** None; food and rest should be airport- or flight-compatible.
