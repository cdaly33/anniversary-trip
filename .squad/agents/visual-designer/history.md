## Seeded Context

- The experience should help Rachel and Chris review anniversary-trip options interactively, with a distinctive visual direction rather than a generic travel dashboard.
- The retained planning directions are Northern Italy + Croatia and Portugal; New Zealand + Australia was structurally rejected in the completed council review.
- Trip guardrails remain 10–12 days, up to 14 when justified; 2 bases preferred and 3 maximum; 1–2 meaningful experiences daily; safe, comfortable, good-value travel over luxury.
- Responsive behavior, keyboard access, legible hierarchy, contrast, motion preferences, and useful non-map fallbacks are part of the design.
- For UI work, the personal frontend-design skill referenced in the charter is required reading but must not be copied into team-visible state.
